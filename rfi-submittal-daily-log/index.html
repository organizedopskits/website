Who it's for

For supers and PMs keeping the paper trail

RFIs go out and the answers come back late. Submittals sit. The daily log is whatever got written down that night. That paper trail matters when there's a question about cost or schedule later.

Here every RFI gets a response due date that sets itself, and overdue ones turn red. Each submittal gets an order-by date that works back from when you need the item on site. The daily log is one row per job per day.

Sample jobs are already filled in so you can see how it works first.
