Builder closing gift FAQ

Can I give it to every buyer?

Yes. Give a copy to each buyer. Please don't resell the blank file.

Where does my company info go?

On the Welcome tab: your logo, phone, email, website and warranty contact. The printable pages also have a fillable From your builder box.

Does it track the 11-month walk-through?

Yes. Enter the closing date and the 11-month walk-through date figures itself.

Do my buyers need Excel?

No. It works in free Google Sheets, and on a phone or iPad through the app.

How is it different from the homeowner planner?

It's built for builders to hand out, with your logo and warranty contact up front. A homeowner buying for their own house can get the home maintenance planner. Real estate agents can get the realtor closing gift.
