Who it's for

Budgets slip one selection at a time

The upgraded faucet. The tile that's $3 more a foot. The lights the client found on Instagram. None of them feel big on their own, but they add up fast.

This selections and allowance tracker is for the builder. It puts every selection next to its allowance so you can see the overage before it turns into an argument at the final invoice.

Questions

Selections and allowance tracker FAQ

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Excel and in free Google Sheets. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets. There are no macros, no subscription and nothing is locked.

Is this for homeowners?

No. It's built for the builder, to track client selections against the allowances in your contract.

How are overages billed?

Each overage becomes a change order with your markup, a CO number and the date it was paid.

Can the client sign off on selections?

Yes. Pick a job and the printable client sign-off sheet fills in with that job's selections.

Is it part of a bundle?

Yes. It's one of the four kits in the Contractor Office Bundle, and it's also sold on its own.
