Who it's for

For GCs who award on more than the bottom line

If you've ever picked the low number and paid for what it left out, this is for you.

Each bid gets a base bid, gap plugs and alternates. The leveled total is all three added up, and rank 1 goes to the lowest leveled total, not the lowest base bid. You also see how far each bid is over the low one.

Sample jobs are already filled in so you can see how it works before you level your own.

Questions

Bid leveling sheet FAQ

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Excel and in free Google Sheets. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets.

Can I use it on my phone or iPad?

Yes. Open the bid leveling sheet in the Google Sheets or Excel app on your phone or iPad. Setup is easier on a computer screen.

How do I get it?

It's an instant download from Etsy. Buy it, download the file and open it. Nothing ships.

Are there macros or a subscription?

No. There are no macros, no subscription and nothing locked. You pay once on Etsy and the file is yours.

How is the leveled total figured?

Leveled total is base bid plus gap plugs plus alternates. Rank 1 is the lowest leveled total, and each bid shows its % over the low one.
