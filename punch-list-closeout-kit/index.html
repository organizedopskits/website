Questions

Punch list and closeout kit FAQ

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Excel and in free Google Sheets. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets. There are no macros, no subscription and nothing is locked.

What files do I get?

Two files: the Excel tracker (which also works in Google Sheets) and the editable Word homeowner manual.

Can I put my logo on the homeowner manual?

Yes. The manual is an editable Word file, so you can add your logo and send it at closing.

How many jobs does the closeout checklist cover?

The 25-step closeout checklist covers up to 6 jobs.

How do warranty claims work?

Log each claim and set its priority. Priority sets the response deadline, and late claims turn red.
