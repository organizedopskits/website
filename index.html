Questions

Frequently asked questions

Do I need Excel?

No. Every kit works in Excel (2007 or newer) and in free Google Sheets. There are no macros.

How do I get my files?

It's an instant download. Etsy gives you the download right after you buy, and it stays in your Etsy purchases. Nothing ships.

Can I use it on my phone or iPad?

Yes, in the Google Sheets or Excel app. Setup is easiest on a computer.

I'm not a spreadsheet person. Will this work for me?

Yes. Each kit has a Quick Start, a filled-in sample copy, and clear white cells for your info. If you get stuck, message me on Etsy.

What's the difference between the two color themes?

Only the colors. Cozy Workshop is cream, blush and sage. Job Site is cream, gray and slate with orange accents. Kits made before the themes launched come in one look.

Can I share the file with my team?

Each purchase is for your own business or home. Message me on Etsy if you need a version for your whole team.
