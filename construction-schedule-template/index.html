Who it's for

For builders who keep the schedule in their head

If your schedule is a whiteboard, a wall calendar or a memory, this is for you. One late task pushes the next one, and keeping every date straight by hand is where things slip.

Here you enter how many workdays a task takes and which task it waits on. The dates work themselves out. Weekends and your holidays get skipped, and the Gantt bars draw themselves.

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Excel and in free Google Sheets. Upload the file to Google Drive and open it with Sheets.

Can I use it on my phone or iPad?

Yes. Open the schedule template in the Google Sheets or Excel app on your phone or iPad. Setup is easier on a computer screen.

It's an instant download from Etsy. Buy it, download the file and open it. Nothing ships.

Are there macros or a subscription?

No. There are no macros, no subscription and nothing locked. You pay once on Etsy and the file is yours.

What happens when a task runs late?

Change its dates and everything that waits on it slides with it. The dashboard shows late tasks and jobs behind their contract date up top.
