Home maintenance checklist FAQ

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Excel (2007 or newer) and in free Google Sheets. There are no macros.

Can I use it on my phone or iPad?

Yes, in the Google Sheets or Excel app.

How do I get it?

It's an instant download. Etsy emails your download link right after you buy. Nothing ships.

Will it skip tasks my home doesn't need?

Yes. Tasks for pools, septic, wells, sprinklers and more are skipped if your home doesn't have them, and cold and hot climate tasks are both included.

Can I share the file?

It's for use in your own home, so please don't resell or share the file. If you run a contractor business, the job costing spreadsheet and the change order template are built for your jobs.
