How it works

Guests RSVP, and the plan follows along

Send the included Google Form with your invite. Replies fill your guest list, and if someone answers twice, the newest reply counts.

The Dashboard shows days to go, who's coming, what's late and what's next. Every task on the countdown checklist has a due date.

There are extra tasks for showers, grad parties and kids parties.

Questions

Party planner spreadsheet FAQ

Do I need Excel?

No. It works in Google Sheets with a free account, and in Excel 2010 or newer, including Mac. There are no macros.

How do RSVPs work?

Guests fill in the included Google Form and their replies fill your guest list. If someone replies twice, the newest reply counts. The RSVP form works in Google Sheets.

Does it work for a party at a venue?

Yes. Pick home or venue at the start, and the checklist, budget and alerts change to fit. You can also compare up to 4 venues by real total.

What do I get?

A blank workbook, a sample workbook, Google Sheets copy links for the planner, sample and RSVP form, 10 printable pages (PDF) and a Start Here page.

Can I use it for my event business?

No. It's for personal use only.
